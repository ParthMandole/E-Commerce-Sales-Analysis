E-Commerce Sales Analysis – Power BI
📊 Project Overview

This project presents an E-Commerce Sales Analysis dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard analyzes sales, profit, customers, orders, regional performance, category performance, and shipping efficiency.

🎯 Project Objectives
Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
Compare sales and profit across different categories.
Analyze regional sales and profit performance.
Understand customer and order volume.
Analyze shipping costs and their impact on profitability.
Identify key business insights from the data.
📌 Key KPIs

1. Sales & Category Analysis

2. Regional Analysis

The dashboard provides regional analysis for:

West
East
Central
Other

It also provides state-level profit analysis to understand geographical performance.

3. Shipping Analysis

The shipping analysis focuses on:

Shipping Cost per Unit
Shipping Cost %
Shipping Cost per Order
Profit After Shipping
Shipping Cost by Category

The dashboard shows a 30.42% shipping cost percentage and approximately $335.81K profit after shipping.

4. Business Insights
Sales and profit are concentrated in fewer categories.
Food and Disposables are major contributors to sales and profit.
Regional performance varies significantly.
Shipping costs can reduce overall profitability.
High-cost categories should be reviewed for shipping efficiency.
Regional concentration may increase business dependency on particular markets.
